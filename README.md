# Dandadan (ダンダダン)

> *"You believe in ghosts, but not aliens?! That doesn't make any sense!"*

**Dandadan** is a high-octane supernatural action-comedy anime series based on the hit manga by Yukinobu Tatsu. The story follows two high schoolers from opposite worlds—a girl who believes in ghosts and a nerdy boy obsessed with aliens—who make a bet to prove each other wrong, only to discover that **both** exist and must team up to survive bizarre paranormal and extraterrestrial threats.

---

### Series Details
* **Original Run:** October 4, 2024 – Present
* **Seasons:** 2 Seasons (24 Episodes total; Season 3 confirmed for 2027)
* **Animation Studio:** Science SARU
* **Genres:** Supernatural Thriller, Action, Romantic Comedy, Sci-Fi
* **Music:** Kensuke Ushio (with iconic opening themes *"Otonoke"* by Creepy Nuts and *"Kakumei Dōchū"* by Aina the End)

### Main Characters

| Character | Powers / Role | Defining Traits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Momo Ayase** | Psychokinesis | A bold, quick-witted gyaru raised by a spirit medium; awakens latent psychic abilities after an alien abduction attempt. |
| **Ken "Okarun" Takakura** | Turbo Granny's Curse | A socially awkward occult nerd who gains explosive speed and a demonic transformation after being cursed by a yokai. |
| **Turbo Granny** | Yokai / Mascot | A foul-mouthed, ultra-fast spirit who steals Okarun's "family jewels" and ends up trapped inside a lucky cat doll. |
| **Seiko Ayase** | Spirit Medium | Momo’s youthful-looking, sharp-tongued grandmother who lends magical barriers and exorcism knowledge to the duo. |
| **Aira Shiratori** | Acrobatic Silky's Aura | A popular, narcissistic classmate with a severe Messiah complex who gains hair-manipulating yokai powers. |
| **Jin "Jiji" Enjoji** | Spiritual Vessel | Momo’s goofy childhood crush whose family home is haunted by a terrifyingly powerful spirit known as the Evil Eye. |

### Why You Should Watch It
1. **Unmatched Visual Energy:** Studio Science SARU brings wild color palettes, fluid sakuga animation, and creative camera angles to every battle.
2. **Genuine Chemistry:** Beneath the absurd comedy and bizarre monster designs is a surprisingly sweet, authentic slow-burn romance between Momo and Okarun.
3. **Emotional Whiplash:** The show seamlessly shifts from laugh-out-loud absurdity to genuinely heartbreaking backstories for the spirits they face.
4. **Top-Tier Soundtrack:** Kensuke Ushio’s eclectic electronic score paired with Creepy Nuts' viral opening makes every episode feel electric.
